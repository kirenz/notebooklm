Departments

Below you will find use case examples for different departments.

Regardless of your specific area, the general process remains the same:

  1. Identify documents relevant to your department
  2. Create a new notebook in NotebookLM
  3. Upload the selected materials
  4. Formulate prompts tailored to your team’s needs
  5. Practice with real scenarios from your daily work

Marketing

NotebookLM can support marketing professionals with campaign planning, brand positioning, and content analysis. Below you’ll find practical examples of how various marketing functions can benefit from public documents and strategic prompts.

TipBrand Management

Use NotebookLM to:

  • Analyze your brand’s language and tone of voice
  • Compare your positioning with that of competitors
  • Extract key statements or value propositions from a page

Suggested Document:

  • Kärcher About Us Page

Example Prompts:

What emotions or messages are emphasized on this page?
How is the brand positioned in terms of innovation and user-friendliness?
TipCampaign Management

Use NotebookLM to:

  • Summarize industry reports and identify campaign-relevant trends
  • Generate messaging ideas based on audience insights
  • Structure creative briefings based on strategic guidelines

Suggested Approach:

Use Gemini Deep Research to create a report. Upload this report to NotebookLM.

Example Prompts:

What are the top marketing trends mentioned in the report?
Which KPIs will become more important in 2025?
